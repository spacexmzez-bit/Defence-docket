Objection Cheat Sheet

During Phase 3, you may interrupt ADA questioning by typing OBJECTION: [Grounds]. If valid, the Judge sustains. If frivolous, you take a Strike.

Hearsay (Rule 802)

Witness testifying about what someone else said out of court to prove it's true.
Exception: Excited utterances or dying declarations.

Speculation (Rule 602)

Witness guessing about things they didn't personally see or experience (e.g., "He must have been angry").

Leading Question (Rule 611c)

The Prosecutor asks a question that suggests the answer (e.g., "You saw him drop the gun, didn't you?"). Only allowed on cross-examination, NOT direct.

Assumes Facts Not in Evidence

Question contains a premise that hasn't been proven yet (e.g., "Where did you hide the wrench?" when a wrench hasn't been mentioned).
